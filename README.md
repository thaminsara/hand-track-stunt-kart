# Hand-Track Stunt Kart
A high-speed 3D multiplayer arcade racing game built with **Ursina (3D)** and **MediaPipe (Hand Tracking)**. Control your car using virtual steering in your webcam view, drift around stunt tracks, hit boost pads, and race your friends over LAN!

## Features
- **Virtual Steering Wheel:** Hold your hands up in front of your webcam. Twist your hands left/right to steer and push/pull or use gestures for throttle and boost!
- **Multiplayer LAN + Local Mode:** Host or join matches on your local network or practice solo.
- **Stunt Arena:** Ramps, loop-de-loops, neon boost pads, and particle spark effects.
- **One-Click Runner:** Automated PowerShell update and launch script.

## Quick Start
Run the PowerShell automation script:
```powershell
.\update-and-run.ps1
```
